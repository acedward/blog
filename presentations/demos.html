Reading the chart

  • Umbra indexing feeds Effectstream too, and nothing else.
  • Umbra feeds Effectstream, and the Offer Files backend runs on Effectstream. Umbra does not reach Offer Files directly.
  • COW Solver is its own project. It talks to the Offer Files backend, and Midnight-Intents sits inside it. Users are reached from there rather than through the Offer Files frontend.
  • UTXO Pinning and Auth Messages are what the frontend needs to build a spend a user can actually sign and settle.
  • AA-V3 reaches Umbra through Metamask, and users can reach AA-V3 through an EVM-Midnight dApp.
  • The dotted line from AA-V3 to the frontend is the open question: whether the frontend can talk to AA-V3 directly rather than only through the backend path.
  • Three lanes end at the user: the Offer Files frontend, the EVM-Midnight dApp, and the COW Solver through Midnight-Intents.
