Design trade-offs

Escrow improves placement but adds state

Pros

  • Offer cost is independent of map-heavy custody growth in the measured fixture.
  • The map-heavy work happens in separate self-balanced transactions.
  • The published file settles through an independent wallet using the stock taker route.
  • The offer transcript falls from 117 to 55 operations.
  • Staging and consolidation remain explicit, inspectable state transitions.

Cons

  • The fixture has one global escrow slot, so only one staged offer exists per Manager.
  • openSwap gives the whole staged coin; partial fill was not measured.
  • There is no cancelStage recovery circuit in the fixture.
  • Received-colour attribution is delayed until consolidation.
  • Productizing the design adds sequencing, liveness, and stuck-state handling.

This is a best-case upper bound for the escrow idea, not the final product contract.

What the result supports

Arm (a) plus arm (e) is a direction, not a measured result

Proven by retained evidence

  • Arm (a) raises the live map-based boundary from 1 to 4 cells.
  • Arm (e) has no observed failure through 16 cells.
  • Arm (e) settles U1 and published-file U2 at 4 cells.
  • Arm (e) fits the measured deployment ceiling at 11 circuits and 50.1%.

Still to design and measure

  • The actual combined arm (a) plus arm (e) contract.
  • A keyed escrow that permits concurrent offers.
  • Cancellation, replacement, expiry, and timeout recovery paths.
  • Partial fills or an explicit whole-coin product rule.
  • Migration and accounting rules for delayed consolidation.

Next step: build the keyed, cancellable product form, then compile, cost, and measure the real combination.

Design takeaway

Move map work off the published offer path. Then fail closed on everything that supports the claim.

01 / Preserve the easy path

Self-merge already works beyond the publication boundary.

02 / Isolate publication

An independent taker needs only a fixed-cost offer plus verifiable bytes.

03 / Productize honestly

Keyed escrow and cancellation must be built and re-measured before claiming the combined design.
